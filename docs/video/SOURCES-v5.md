# Video v5 industry-stat sources

Checked **November 7, 2026**. Spoken narration does not name the publisher or a
calendar year. The proof card at **0:20–0:26** carries the source, publication date
and actual data period on screen.

[Foureyes: 2026 Automotive Dealer Benchmarks Report](https://www.foureyes.io/blog/2026-automotive-dealer-benchmarks-report)
was published April 22, 2026 and analyses January–December 2025 website activity,
including more than 22,900 dealership/group websites. The screen shows:

- **42.7%** of qualified leads mishandled. The population is qualified dealership
  website leads, not every enquiry or every customer in the automotive market.
- **61.2%** purchasing within three days of their initial website enquiry, conditional
  on the lead eventually purchasing. This is purchase/close timing, not a measure
  of when every buyer makes a decision.

currently reviews imported CSV leads; the demo does not capture unlogged leads,
accelerate first response, send customer messages or prove a recovered sale.

No industry-wide lost-revenue estimate or percentage-recovery claim is added.
